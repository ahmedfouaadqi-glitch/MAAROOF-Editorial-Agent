# Platform Registry — Initial Classification

Source: user-provided CSV (17 rows). The platform descriptions below preserve the supplied content-purpose labels. Operational classifications are conservative engineering metadata, not claims that a platform currently accepts automated publishing.

## Editorial destinations
- Medium — professional articles, experiences, analysis and opinions.
- Substack — specialist articles and newsletters.
- WordPress — blogs and knowledge sites.
- Blogger — general articles and blogs.
- Ghost — independent publishing and digital magazines.

## Professional and technical communities
- DEV Community — programming and technical engineering.
- Hashnode — developer and technology articles.
- LinkedIn Articles — professional and business articles.

## Community Q&A
- Reddit — discussions, experiences and user opinions.
- Quora — knowledge questions and answers.
- Stack Exchange — specialist technical and scientific answers.

## Knowledge and research
- Wikipedia — general encyclopedic knowledge.
- arXiv — scientific and technical research.
- PubMed Central — published medical research.

## Developer assets and data sources
- GitHub — code, projects and documentation.
- Common Crawl — large web-page crawl archive.
- Hugging Face Datasets — model-training datasets.

## Operational rules
1. Do not treat all 17 entries as magazine destinations.
2. Official APIs are preferred where available and properly authorized.
3. Browser automation requires a platform-specific adapter, a policy review, and a successful controlled test.
4. Community and Q&A platforms must not be used as promotional article-dumping channels.
5. Wikipedia, arXiv, PubMed Central, Common Crawl, and dataset repositories are not general-purpose press-release outlets.
6. Status remains unverified until a documented test is completed.
